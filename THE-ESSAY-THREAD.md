## The two laws this thread runs on

1. **The mouth is only the residue** (penelope's house law, mouth-last): the
   field remembers (read), the hunt goes and gets (addresses), and the mouth
   is asked only for what neither had — the phrasing and ordering of an essay
   whose facts and quotes the record already holds.
2. **No heimdall needed** (the user's correction): the fold's own surface is
   built to run the model in the page (WebLLM). The server only moves data
   the record produced; the model moves only the prose. The bytes never cross
   a model's mouth without a mechanical snip on the way back.

## The honest caveat

The mouth drew a TRUTH here ("Pierre is the one the record keeps returning
to") and the box grounded it (Pierre×82) — but the general guarantee is
structural, not semantic: penelope SEALS what the box can vouch, and REFUSES
what it cannot. A mouth that drifts from the box is refused at the seal, and
the refusal is shown, never hidden.
