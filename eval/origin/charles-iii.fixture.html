Charles was created Prince of Wales and Earl of Chester on 26 July 1958,[32] although his investiture did not take place until 1 July 1969, when he was crowned by his mother in a televised ceremony at Caernarfon Castle.[33] The event was controversial in Wales amid rising Welsh nationalist sentiment.[34] He took his seat in the House of Lords the following year,[35] delivering his maiden speech on 13 June 1974,[36] the first royal to speak from the floor since the future Edward VII in 1884.[37] He addressed the House again in 1975.[38]
