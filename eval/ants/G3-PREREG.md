## The rule I will build (frozen before the corpus is scored)
`fold-chat-anaphora.js` (new, pure, no model): `anaphoraOf(question, {lang, fw})` -> `{ carry: true|false, why, lang }`.
carry = true ONLY when the ask is anaphoric or elliptical:
 (a) it has no content word of its own after removing the language's closed class (functionWordsOf), the question/request frame words and a closed "continuation" class (more/else/further/again…): "tell me more", "why?", "and then?", "really?";
 (b) it opens with a connective/elliptical frame ("and", "but", "what about", "how about", "also"; es/fr equivalents) followed by <= 3 content words and no verb-frame of its own: "and in 1999?", "and her husband?";
 (c) it contains an anaphoric pronoun/demonstrative of the language's closed class ("he she it they him her them his its their this that these those one(s)"; es/fr lists) and is NOT a self-sufficient ask (names an entity of its own, or is >= 9 segments), minus a short closed list of EXPLETIVE frames ("it takes", "it is possible/true/safe", "it rains"…).
Otherwise -> carry = false. Language with no closed-class prior (functionWordsOf null) -> carry = false, `why: "undecided: no closed-class prior for <lang>"` (a typed gap).
Wiring in my own files: `followUp` (fold-chat-thread.js) asks the gate before ANY carry path (pronoun/`carried`, comparative/elliptical variant); a standalone verdict returns `kind: "standalone"` with a `why`. `carryNotice`, `applyCarry` stay as they are except `applyCarry` takes the verdict (when the turn did not carry, the summary's Flow/Entities are not rebuilt from the last topic).
Style carry: `fold-chat-histkind.js` (new, pure): `styleSafeHistory(history/messages, {kind, classify})` — for a research/chat/advice turn, a prior turn whose ask classifies as generate/compose (or whose reply is creative) is replaced by ONE marker line (the person asked for a song earlier) or dropped; a generate turn after a generate turn keeps its history. Marker vs drop is chosen by the real-model run below, not by taste.
