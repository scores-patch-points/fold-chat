# C8 pre-registration (written 2026-10-06 BEFORE any run of primary-v2.mjs, any decoy, any measurement of v2)

Task: A3 measured fold-chat-primary.js (A2) at reach 1/14 vs baseline 9/14 because its gate refuses true sentences on wording and its candidate generator drops headings/fragments. C8 builds a COPY (eval/ants/c8/primary-v2.mjs), fixes the gate mechanically, and measures on A3's cached web snapshot (eval/ants/.primary-cache.json, eval/ants/primary-corpus.json), both wordings.

## What I had seen before writing this (disclosure)
A3-RESULTS.md (the boss quoted its diagnosis), fold-chat-primary.js, fold-chat-provenance.js, the ground.js figure/unit machinery. I had NOT run v2, nor looked at the text of any cached page, nor re-run A2. The rules below are therefore designed from A3's written diagnosis, not from the page text; the A3 corpus is the DEVELOPMENT set for reach (in-sample; there is no held-out corpus, and I say so in the results). The decoys below are written before any run and are the out-of-sample check; they are frozen in c8/decoys.json (md5 recorded in the results).

## The rules (each gets a test and a mutation check)
- (a) SPELL: stems are spelling-folded before stemming: -re/-res -> -er/-ers after a consonant (metre, centre), -our -> -or (colour; with a small exclusion list: hour, four, your, tour, pour, sour, devour, detour, contour), -ize/-yze family -> -ise/-yse.
- (b) HEAD-NOUN SLACK, narrow: a claim stem missing from the sentence may be forgiven only if ALL hold: it is a LOWERCASE (not a name) content word of the claim's SUBJECT region (the tokens before the claim's first copula/auxiliary/have), it is not the last content word of that region (the head), the head IS in the sentence, the claim states at least one figure and EVERY figure matches (rule c/d), the claim's main predicate stem (first content non-figure stem after the cue, or its declared equivalent) is present, and at most 2 stems are forgiven. Names (capitalised words of the claim that are not sentence-initial) are NEVER forgiven by slack. Declared equivalents (a tiny table, declared not measured): win ~ award/receive/grant; senator ~ member; also kin by the page: a missing NAME stem is supplied only by the PAGE'S OWN title/host/path words (the page IS about the entity), never by the sentence's neighbours.
- (c) NAME-BOUND FIGURES: a figure of the claim directly after a capitalised word (Apollo 11, Windows 95) is part of the NAME: the sentence must carry that name followed by that figure (stem+figure adjacent), and the bare figure elsewhere ("11 days") does not satisfy it. Such a figure is not a quantity.
- (d) UNIT EQUIVALENCE: a claim figure with a unit (km, metres, miles, feet...) is satisfied by the same quantity in another declared unit within 1% (384,400 km ~ 238,855 miles); same-unit figures stay exact; a figure without a unit stays exact. Uses ground.figuresIn units table.
- (e) CANDIDATES: in addition to what provenance.candidatesFor offers, the page's short declarative fragments and list items (>= 3 words, <= 220 chars, not a nav/link run) and HEADING + FOLLOWING-SENTENCE pairs are offered when they carry a figure of the claim; a pair or fragment still faces the full gate (the heading is part of the candidate text, so a heading naming another entity cannot lend its entity).

## What stays (the hard rule)
The host tables (HOST_KINDS drop, MIRROR_HOSTS, ENCYCLOPEDIAS), the text-copy test, the assertion gate (neg/deny/hedge/question parity), and "every FIGURE must be present" are unchanged except as (c)(d) define. Names never slack. The model still only POINTS BY NUMBER.

## The 20 decoys (frozen in c8/decoys.json before any run)
6 wrong figure, 6 wrong entity, 4 negation/denial, 4 outdated holder or count. Each is a plausible sentence that a page could carry, offered against the claim it attacks, in BOTH wordings. Two ways in: (i) gate level: the decoy sentence as the pointed candidate; (ii) page level: a synthetic page on an official-looking host carrying the decoy (some with a heading) and a WORST-CASE pointer that picks the decoy candidate whenever it is offered. A decoy counts as ACCEPTED if findPrimary returns it under either wording. Plus 8 "mirror" pages carrying the TRUE sentence on britannica/wikiwand/dbpedia/fandom/quora/*pedia hosts and a verbatim copy of the encyclopedia text: all must be rejected.
Known-limit decoys (reported, NOT counted, expected to pass any stem gate, both before and after): "The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization was founded on 24 October 1945." (a different body, same words) and tense ("Emmanuel Macron was the president of France.").

## Bars (declared)
1. Decoys accepted = 0 of 20 (after any rule revert), and mirror pages accepted = 0 of 8. A rule whose addition lets a decoy through is REVERTED (reported as reverted).
2. Reach (oracle, A3's own, headline arm a2idx wording both) with the real model gemma2:2b temp 0: >= 9/14 in at least one wording and no worse than A2 in the other, false accepts (forbidden page, or a returned quote that does not match the claim's `say` regexes) <= 1 page in 14 per wording.
3. Every new rule has a unit test and a mutation (delete the rule, a test fails).
## Predictions

With gemma2:2b pointing, reach 5-8/14 per wording, because 6 of A3's 15 withdrawals were the model saying NONE or picking the wrong sentence, which no gate fix touches. ## What would refute me
Any decoy accepted after the rules are in (that rule goes); a v2 false accept on the oracle's `say` check of the quote; reach not above A2's 1/14 (then the gate was not the bottleneck); model-free reach <= model reach (then the pointing step is not the bottleneck and I misread A3).
