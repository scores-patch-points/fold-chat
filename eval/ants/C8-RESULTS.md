## What the rules are now (all in the copy)
(a) `spellFold`: -re/-res -> -er/-ers, -our -> -or (exclusion list hour/four/tour/...), -ize/-yze -> -ise/-yse, before stemming. (b) narrow slack: a lowercase non-name word of the claim's subject (before its first is/has/...) may be absent only if every figure is satisfied, at most 2 such words, a subject anchor is present, and the sentence's own subject uses only the claim's words; names never. (c) a figure after a capitalised word ("Apollo 11") must appear as that name+figure. (d) a figure with a unit is satisfied by the same quantity in another unit within 1% (and the same number in another unit is NOT the same figure). (e) candidates: short fragments / list items and heading+next-sentence pairs (one verbatim slice) that carry every claim figure and half the stems. Additions beyond the brief, each with a test and mutant: declared equivalents (won~awarded/received, senators~members, tall~height/high, founded~began/established/created; a table, declared not measured), "U.S./US/USA" = United States, a claim's SUBJECT name may be supplied by the page's own title/host/path only when the sentence names its own subject in the claim's words, the unit word of a converted figure is not required, an optional `prefilter` (the model is shown only candidates that already pass the gate; its pick is checked again; default OFF), declarative-first ordering under prefilter, and `fragmentMinRank: 3` (heading/fragment/pair candidates only from government/education/agency/organisation hosts; an unclassified host keeps provenance's sentence candidates only).
Kept unchanged: host tables and drops, mirror text-copy test, assertion gate (neg/deny/hedge/question parity), every FIGURE required, the model only points by number and its pick is gated.

## Why the other 7 (lean) are not reached, from the trails (not a fix)
head-of-state, capital, height-everest, museum-monalisa: the cached search results hold no official host page that states it (elysee.fr's cached Macron page is in the snapshot but was not among the results; capital/Everest/Mona Lisa results are all tourism/blog hosts): a FINDER limit, not the gate. founding-harvard: harvard.edu's timeline has only the fragment "1636: First College in American colonies founded." (no subject); the earlier, looser supply rule accepted it, I tightened it because the same rule also accepted a ticket seller's "This is how..." sentence for the Mona Lisa: a designed refusal. definition-tsunami: NOAA says "a large and sudden displacement of the ocean", the claim says "a large volume of water": an honest refusal (different words, no figure, no slack). event-apollo11: nasa.gov pages carry "Apollo 11" and "July 20, 1969" in different sentences, so a sentence-level check cannot confirm (page-level co-occurrence, the baseline's reach test, can). The claim (detailed) wording fails mostly on detail the pages do not state ("Sancellemoz sanatorium", "two for each of the 50 states", "81-storey", "capital CITY"), which the gate rightly requires: one candidate rule not built: a trailing class word of a predicate ("city" in "capital city") as a forgivable qualifier (nca.gov.au was refused for exactly `missing_stem:city`).

## What a person should judge (the cost side)
- v2 returns MORE pages from hosts the oracle does not list (lean 9 vs A2's 7; baseline 3). The two newly returned pages in the lean arm (A2 had the other 7: onthisday, remitly x2 incl. oreate.ai, researchinpoland, the tsunami education site, landmarkevents, eiffeltowertravel) are cuip.org (an advocacy group's "exactly 100 voting members of the United States Senate", via senators~members) and alpineramble.com (a title "The new height of Mount Everest 8,848.86 meters"); in the claim wording nhm.ac.uk (the Natural History Museum: arguably a fair secondary source) is new (A2 also returned hbmag.com, onthisday and the tsunami site there). All judged non-primary by A3's standard. This is A3's finding unchanged by C8: `origin` tier admits any host that is not on a drop list. The gate now makes those hosts more likely to PASS, so the host question (an allow-list of what may be labelled "verified at origin") is the next fix and is not in this diff. `fragmentMinRank: 3` keeps headings/fragments/pairs away from unclassified hosts; unrestricted, lean reach was the same 7 and the unlisted pages 10 instead of 9 (superseded2/ run).
- Some quotes are headings or titles, not sentences ("Harvard University Is Founded, 1636", "Eiffel Tower Height: 330m (1,083 ft) ..."), from provenance's own candidate list on unclassified hosts: the same as A2 had.
- A pair quote reads "Heading Sentence" run together (a verbatim slice across a line break, whitespace-collapsed); mergeProvenance/verifyNarration accept it (tested), but whether the app should show it that way is a product call.
- The equivalents table and the "subject name supplied by the page's own title" rule are judgement calls (declared, not measured). The 2 of 7 reach that depend on supply: usa.gov (the Senate; title "U.S. Senate | USAGov"). Without supply it is `missing_stem:unit,stat`.

## What this does NOT measure
- IN-SAMPLE: the rules were designed from A3's written diagnosis and tuned while looking at this snapshot's trails (the corpus is the development set; there is no held-out one). Reach 7/14 is therefore optimistic. The 20 decoys (written before any run, never tuned) and 18 mirror pages are the out-of-sample part, and they test rejection only.
- One web snapshot (A3's), 14 claims, English only, one 2b pointing model. No browser path (A3's caveat stands), no re-search; the baseline is A3's own run, not re-measured.
- Decoys are single sentences I wrote; a sentence naming the right entity with a wrong detail the claim does not state, or a different body with the same words (K1), passes any stem gate.
- The wiring (replace the file, flip `prefilter`, edit 6 tests) is the boss's; nothing outside eval/ants/ and c8/ was touched.

## Development history (disclosure, because it bears on the numbers)
Runs 1-2 (superseded/, then superseded2/) were on earlier code; what changed after seeing them and why: the supply rule was tightened three times (page title no longer lends subject words; a sentence with no named subject cannot be supplied; only the claim's SUBJECT names, never its predicate names) after in-sample quotes showed ticket-seller sentences passing; `fragmentMinRank` was added after the unlisted-host count rose. Lean reach was 7/14 in all three; claim reach 2/14 in all. Decoys were never changed and no decoy ever passed v2.
